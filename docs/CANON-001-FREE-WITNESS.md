# CANON-001 — FREE WITNESS

## Governing law

> **WITNESS does not distribute Scripture among people.  
> People approach Scripture freely.  
> WITNESS preserves what they leave there and composes from abundance.**

This is a canonical architectural law for WITNESS.

## Consequence

The core primitive is not an assignment.

It is:

```
PERSON
  contributed
RECORDING
  of
PASSAGE
  using
TEXT EDITION
```

No contributor needs to claim a verse before reading it.

No verse needs to be reserved.

No reader is responsible for completing a chapter.

No recording replaces another merely because it addresses the same passage.

A person may read any passage they want, wherever and whenever they want, subject to contribution and rights rules.

## Abundance is valid data

If one passage has one witness and another has one thousand, WITNESS does not treat that imbalance as corruption.

Repeated human return to a passage is itself part of the witness corpus.

Coverage and abundance are different measurements:

- **coverage** — which passage addresses have at least one eligible recording;
- **abundance** — how many distinct eligible recordings exist at an address.

Neither is silently optimized into the other.

## Composition belongs to listening

Contributors leave witnessed recordings.

Listening engines compose those recordings into traversals.

A chapter, book, or whole-Bible playback may therefore be assembled from many independent people without any contributor having been assigned a role in that assembly.

Possible assembly policies include:

- community mosaic;
- one witness as far as their recordings extend;
- recordings the listener has not heard before;
- language-specific traversal;
- chronological traversal;
- declared family or community sets;
- lawful random selection among eligible witnesses.

An assembly policy selects among preserved witnesses. It does not promote one recording into the canonical text or erase alternatives.

## Rooms are optional gatherings

A WITNESS room is a social convenience, not an authority boundary for Scripture.

A room may gather people who want to contribute together, but it must not require verse assignment as the default ontology.

The earlier two-reader alternating-verse prototype remains a useful experiment in collaborative assembly. It is not the governing contribution model.

## Canon boundary

The textual witness / edition remains distinct from all contributed audio.

Human recordings accumulate **around** passage addresses.

They do not silently edit Scripture.

## Compression

> **People choose what to read.  
> WITNESS remembers.  
> The listener composes.**
